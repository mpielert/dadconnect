**Subject:** Want to help me test CMUDadConnect?

---

Hi [Name],

I've been building a small private site for our group — **CMUDadConnect** — and it's far enough along that I'd love a few of you to kick the tires before I open it up to everyone.

It's invite-only, so I've already created an account for you. Nothing to sign up for.

**How to get in**

1. Go to **https://cmudadconnect.com**
2. Click **Sign in** and enter this email address ([their email])
3. You'll get an email with a one-time sign-in link — click it and you're in

No password to create or remember. Every time you sign in, you just get a fresh link by email. (If the link doesn't show up in a minute, check your spam folder — and tell me, because that's useful to know.)

The first time you sign in, you'll set up your profile. Everything except your name is optional, and **you control what's shared** — each field has its own "share with group" checkbox, so you can fill in your city but keep it private if you'd rather.

**What's on the site**

There are four sections:

- **Directory** — who's in the group, with whatever details each person chose to share. You can also add your kids here (name and age only — kids don't get logins, and nothing else about them is ever stored).
- **Career** — opt in as a "resource" if you're open to giving advice, making intros, or doing a mock interview. Anyone can then send you a specific request, and you can accept, decline, or point them to someone better suited. Mainly aimed at helping our kids.
- **Crash Pads** — set whether you're open to hosting someone passing through your city (yes / maybe / no, plus any constraints like "couch only"). Others can request to stay, and you can accept, decline, or counter-propose. **No addresses are stored anywhere** — that's on purpose; you'd share that directly only if you accept.
- **Travel** — post trip notes and recommendations, reply to each other's, and add photos. If you post about a city where someone's open to hosting, the site quietly points that out.

**A couple of honest caveats**

- There's no messaging yet — anywhere the site implies "contact this person," that part isn't built. It's the next thing I'm planning, and I'd like your read on how it should work.
- You're among the very first people in, so it'll feel empty until a few of you add things. Posting something is more useful than just looking around.
- Please use real-ish info but nothing you'd be upset to see on a screen — it's a real site, but very much a work in progress.

**What I'd love to hear back**

Poke around for 15–20 minutes, then answer whatever you have opinions about:

1. **Getting in** — was the email sign-in obvious, or did anything make you hesitate?
2. **First impression** — after your first minute, was it clear what the site is *for*?
3. **Profile & privacy** — did you feel in control of what you were sharing? Was anything unclear about what's public to the group?
4. **The four sections** — which one would you actually use? Which feels pointless?
5. **Kids** — if you added a child, did that feel right? Anything about it you'd want done differently?
6. **Crash Pads** — would you genuinely set a hosting status? Does the no-address approach feel reassuring or annoying?
7. **Career** — would you opt in as a resource? If our kids used one thing here, what should it be?
8. **What's missing?** — the one thing that would make you come back, and anything that felt broken or confusing.
9. **Would you use this?** — honestly. "Probably not" is a useful answer.

Anything that felt clunky, ugly, or confusing is exactly what I want to know — don't be polite about it.

Thanks for doing this.
